Gallery Controls

PHOTO gallery supports MOUSE CONTROL.
HOVER to view photo date and caption.
CLICK to expand into modal.

PHOTO modal supports MOUSE AND KEYBOARD CONTROL.
CLICK RIGHT 1/3 or PRESS RIGHT ARROW to show next photo.
CLICK LEFT 1/3 or PRESS LEFT ARROW to show previous photo.
CLICK CENTER 1/3 or PRESS ESC to close modal.
